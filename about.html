Who We Are

A Place Where Potential Becomes Progress

Hope High Academy is dedicated to supporting children's educational development in a welcoming and encouraging environment.

Through our Nursery, Primary and Secondary sections, we aim to help learners develop essential knowledge, practical skills, confidence, discipline and respect for others.

We believe education is about more than passing examinations. It is also about developing responsible individuals who can contribute positively to their families and communities.

Our Journey

Growing Since 2014

From a nursery and primary school to a full school with its own examination centre and its first graduating class.

2014

Nursery and Primary Began

Hope High Academy opened its Nursery and Primary sections.

2020

Secondary Section Started

The Secondary section was added so learners could continue their education with us.

2026

Own Examination Centre

The school became an examination centre, so students can sit their exams in their own school.

2026

First SS3 Graduates

Our first set of SS3 students graduated, a proud milestone for the whole school.

Class of 2026

Our First Set of Graduates

In 2026 Hope High Academy graduated its first set of SS3 students, a proud moment for our learners, parents and staff.
